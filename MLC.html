MCL Recruitment Standards

Our Professional Principles

MCL maintains high standards throughout the recruitment process to support candidate success.

Verified Qualifications

All cruise candidates are thoroughly screened to help ensure they hold the necessary STCW and role-specific qualifications required for their intended position.

Safety awareness, professional conduct, and role-specific competence are important parts of preparing candidates for work at sea.

Fair & Transparent Contracts

Employment information should clearly outline applicable terms, wages, working hours, accommodation, living conditions, and other relevant conditions.

Career Guidance

CV & Interview Tips

Prepare yourself for success with these professional recommendations.

CV Tips

Keep your CV professional and easy to read with clear formatting.
Clearly show relevant experience in hospitality, maritime, or customer service.
Include all relevant certifications and qualifications.
Use accurate and up-to-date contact information.
Highlight kitchen, housekeeping, technical, or guest service experience.
Avoid unnecessary information — keep it focused and relevant.
Check spelling and grammar carefully before submitting.

Interview Tips

Join your Zoom interview early — punctuality matters.
Test your camera and microphone before the interview begins.
Dress professionally as you would for an in-person interview.
Keep your passport and certificates available during the interview.
Answer questions honestly and with confidence.
Be familiar with your own CV — know what you've submitted.
Do not pay unauthorized people promising guaranteed employment.
